03 / Audit methodology

What I would examine

Technical & site structure

  • Indexability, sitemap, robots rules, HTTPS and canonical URLs.
  • Mobile usability, page performance and image loading.
  • Navigation between collections, brands, models and products.
  • Duplicate, unavailable and out-of-stock product URL handling.

Product content & credibility

  • Unique titles, useful descriptions and non-duplicated product copy.
  • Accurate model/reference terminology and image alt text.
  • Visible seller identity, contact, policies and secure checkout.
  • Evidence behind authenticity, provenance, condition and servicing claims.

Possible tools for a real audit: Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights, a crawler, structured-data validators and a keyword research spreadsheet. I would request authorized access where needed.

08 / Measurement

What success could look like

Search visibility

  • Search Console impressions, clicks and query mix
  • Organic landing-page trends
  • Indexing and page experience issues

Qualified business activity

  • Qualified enquiries and appointment requests
  • Product-detail engagement and add-to-cart, if applicable
  • Organic conversion rate and revenue, when properly tracked

Establish a baseline and compare meaningful periods. Traffic, enquiries and sales depend on inventory, market conditions, pricing, trust and many other factors. This concept reports no outcomes.

SEO for luxury resale is also a trust experience.
