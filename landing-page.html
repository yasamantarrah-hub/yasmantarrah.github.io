01 / Landing page concept

02 / Conversion approach

Build reassurance into the customer journey.

01 · DISCOVER

Clear positioning

Define the offer and audience quickly: curated pre-owned watches, transparent listing details.

02 · EXPLORE

Useful navigation

Let shoppers filter by brand, model family, price range and availability where supported.

03 · EVALUATE

Evidence, not hype

Show condition, reference, included items, provenance and service records only when documented.

04 · ACT

Easy next step

Offer clear contact, viewing, delivery, payment and return information before commitment.

03 / UX & business strategy

What the page needs to accomplish

Primary content blocks

  • Brand proposition and curated inventory entry point
  • Current, real product cards with availability
  • Buying guide: condition, servicing and paperwork
  • About the seller, contact and policies
  • Editorial stories that answer buyer questions

Trust & usability guardrails

  • Use real photographs and accurate item descriptions
  • State seller identity and independence clearly
  • Explain inspection and authentication process precisely
  • Make fees, shipping, returns and warranty understandable
  • Optimize mobile speed, accessible contrast and keyboard use

05 / How I would measure it

Track meaningful actions—not vanity metrics alone.

Discovery

  • Organic clicks and impressions by landing page
  • Queries and device mix
  • Engagement with collection and product pages

Commercial quality

  • Qualified product enquiries
  • Appointment requests or checkout starts, if offered
  • Conversion rate and revenue with appropriate analytics
