Portfolio case study · Keyword research

Turn search intent into a content strategy for luxury resale.

A keyword and editorial planning concept for Maison Seconde, a fictional online vintage and pre owned luxury watch retailer. The strategy maps shopper questions to useful pages throughout the research, evaluation and buying journey.

Scope note: This is a strategic draft, not completed keyword research. Keyword examples are seed ideas only. Search volumes, competition, SERP features and actual rankings have not been verified.
01 / Business objective

Attract relevant visitors and help them make informed decisions.

GOAL 01

Be discoverable

Align category and product pages with realistic shopping searches.

GOAL 02

Earn consideration

Publish trustworthy guidance for shoppers researching high value pre owned pieces.

GOAL 03

Support conversion

Connect educational content to relevant inventory, clear policies and enquiry options.

02 / Audience & intent

Understand the questions behind the query.

Potential audiences

  • Enthusiasts comparing references and design details
  • First time buyers seeking confidence and clarity
  • Collectors researching condition, provenance and service records
  • Gift buyers evaluating budget, delivery and return terms

Strategy principle

  • Give each important page one clear purpose
  • Separate purchase ready pages from informational guides
  • Do not create thin pages for every keyword variation
  • Use only factual, substantiated product information

04 / Site architecture

Make the content easy to navigate.

  • /watches/ · main pre owned inventory hub
  • /watches/brand/ · brand collections only where stock and useful content justify them
  • /watches/model or reference/ · product detail pages for real, distinct items
  • /journal/buying a vintage watch/ · evergreen buyer guide
  • /journal/watch condition guide/ · condition and terminology guide
  • /journal/service history and documents/ · educational resource
  • /buying/returns delivery warranty/ · practical policy information

These are proposed URL patterns, not live pages. The final structure should reflect the real catalogue, platform and customer needs.

05 / Editorial calendar

A practical first month

WEEK 01

Research & map

Review search results, competitors, customer questions and available inventory. Validate topic priorities.

WEEK 02

Build core pages

Improve collection copy, product information templates and internal navigation.

WEEK 03

Publish buyer guide

Create a helpful guide to assessing a pre owned watch, with transparent limitations and expert review as needed.

WEEK 04

Measure & refine

Check indexing, Search Console data, engagement and enquiries. Choose the next content update based on evidence.

06 / On page content brief

Example article: “How to assess a vintage watch listing”

Reader needs

  • Know what product specifications to check
  • Understand condition terminology
  • Ask informed questions about servicing and documentation
  • Understand return protections and seller disclosures

Suggested outline

  • Start with the exact reference and listing details
  • Review photos and condition notes
  • Understand service records and included accessories
  • Questions to ask the seller
  • Compare policies before purchase

Editorial standard: avoid presenting paperwork, appearance or a seller statement as conclusive proof of authenticity. For high value purchases, direct readers toward qualified independent verification and clear consumer protections.

07 / Internal links & calls to action

Connect learning with shopping without pressure.

  • Buying guide → link to relevant current collection, where appropriate
  • Condition guide → link to listing condition details and policy information
  • Product listing → link to seller process, returns and contact details
  • Journal pages → use descriptive anchor text, not repetitive exact match links
  • Never promote unavailable inventory as currently for sale
08 / Measurement framework

Evaluate the strategy using real data.

VisibilitySearch Console impressions, clicks and query trends
Content usefulnessRelevant engagement and paths into product pages
Business impactQualified enquiries, appointment requests or sales where trackable

Set a baseline before changes. Review trends over meaningful periods, segment by page type and avoid claiming SEO causation without appropriate analysis.

Project takeaway: The approach combines keyword research, site architecture, helpful editorial content, product page quality and measurement. To make this a completed portfolio case study, validate the query set, cite the research date and tools, and document what was actually implemented.
